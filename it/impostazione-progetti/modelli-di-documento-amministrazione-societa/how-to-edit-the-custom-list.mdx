---
title: "Come modificare una lista personalizzata"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **applicazione web**

Log in nell' **Area Amministrazione Societa'**

<img src="/images/direct/1521177204174-Admin_Website.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.001" />

Vai a **Ispezioni Societa'** &gt; **Liste**

### **1. Modifica Nome Lista**

Cliccare sulla matita<img src="/images/direct/1523848840678-Screen-Shot-2018-04-16-at-11.19.54-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 16 at 11.19.54 AM" width="26" /> per modificare il **nome Lista**.

<img src="/images/direct/1523850491638-Admin_Websiteeee.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Websiteeee.001" />

<img src="/images/direct/1523850514713-Admin_Websiteeee.002.jpeg" alt="Admin Websiteeee.002" />

### 2. Modifica Elementi Lista

Clicca Modifica elementi Lista.

<img src="/images/direct/1523850541986-Admin_Websiteeee.003.jpeg" alt="Admin Websiteeee.003" />

Cliccare **icona**<img src="/images/direct/1523848840678-Screen-Shot-2018-04-16-at-11.19.54-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 16 at 11.19.54 AM" width="26" /> per modificare item.

<img src="/images/direct/1523850608498-Admin_Websiteeee.004.jpeg" alt="Admin Websiteeee.004" />

<img src="/images/direct/1523850629826-Admin_Websiteeee.005.jpeg" alt="Admin Websiteeee.005" />

### 3. Aggiungi elementi Lista

Clicca **Aggiungi** **item Lista**

<img src="/images/direct/1524724551076-1523850608498-Admin_Websiteeee.004.jpeg" alt="1523850608498 Admin Websiteeee.004" />

<img src="/images/direct/1523849567158-exclamation-mark.png" alt="exclamation mark" width="23" />E' possibile modificare elementi lista ma **non cancellarli** e **non e' possibile modificare l' ordine di visualizzazione****.**
