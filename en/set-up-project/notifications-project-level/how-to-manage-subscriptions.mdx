---
title: "How to manage subscriptions?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

To edit subscriptions for yourself, go to My Subscriptions. Click Edit to make changes, or click Unsubscribe to remove yourself from a subscription others have set up for you, or a subscription you have set up for yourself.

<img src="/images/uploads/129614-Admin_Website.061.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.061" />

To edit subscriptions for all, go to Project Administration &gt; Subscriptions. Click Edit to make changes, or click Remove to get rid of the entire subscription.

<img src="/images/uploads/129613-Admin_Website.060.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.060" />
