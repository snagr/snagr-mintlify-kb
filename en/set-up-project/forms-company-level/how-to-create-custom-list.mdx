---
title: "How to create a custom list?"
---

Custom list is a constrained drop down list.

While it makes it easier for people to complete a form, it also allows your company to collect structured data from a form.

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

First, login to **Company Admin Area**. Only company admin can see this button.

<img src="/images/direct/1521177204174-Admin_Website.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.001" />

Go to **Company Inspections** ＞ **Lists**

You can choose **Launch Import Wizard** or **Add List Manually**.

Note: If you have more than 10 items to add, please jump to Launch Import Wizard directly.

### 1. Option A - Add List Manually

### <img src="/images/direct/1521177288125-Admin_Website.002.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.002" />

Input **List Name** &gt; **Submit**

<img src="/images/direct/1521177316055-Admin_Website.003.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.003" />

Click **Add List Item(s)**

<img src="/images/direct/1521177638571-Admin_Website.004.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.004" />

Input the option one by one, click **Add Item** then input next option.

<img src="/images/direct/1521177728233-Admin_Website.005.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.005" />

Click **Close** if completed.

<img src="/images/direct/1521177793473-Admin_Website.006.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.006" />

Please scroll down further to see &gt; **Set form fields to show custom list**.

### 1. Option B - Launch Import Wizard

<img src="/images/direct/1521177920679-Admin_Website.007.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.007" />

**Download template** ＞ Create custom list in Excel &gt; **Upload template** &gt; **Import Lists**

<img src="/images/direct/1521177977819-Admin_Website.008.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.008" />

Your Excel file should look like this:

Column A is the name of the custom list. Column B is the display text / options in the custom list.

<img src="/images/direct/1521178106666-Admin_Website.009.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.009" />

<img src="/images/direct/1521178138786-Admin_Website.010.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.010" />

### 2. Set the form field to show custom list

**Return** to the form wizard &gt; **Select** a form field

<img src="/images/direct/1524713479334-demo40.snagr.com.hk_Admin_form-wizard-1-.png" alt="demo40.snagr.com.hk Admin form wizard (1)" />

Click **_Command + 4_** or **_Ctrl + 4_** to bring up **Properties**.

Go to **Type,** change Text to List.

<img src="/images/direct/1524714045215-Screen-Shot-2018-04-26-at-11.39.23-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 26 at 11.39.23 AM" />

<img src="/images/direct/1524713840306-Screen-Shot-2018-04-26-at-11.32.27-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 26 at 11.32.27 AM" />

Afterwards, don't forget to **assign a list** to the field. In this case, we choose 1-12 as this was the one we just created.

<img src="/images/direct/1524713946034-Screen-Shot-2018-04-26-at-11.33.34-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 26 at 11.33.34 AM" />

Last, don't forget to click **_Command + S_** or **_Ctrl + S_** to **s****ave** the changes you made in form wizard.

When new forms are created, you should now see the list:

<img src="/images/direct/1521171629566-Screen-Shot-2018-03-16-at-11.34.36-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 03 16 at 11.34.36 AM" />
