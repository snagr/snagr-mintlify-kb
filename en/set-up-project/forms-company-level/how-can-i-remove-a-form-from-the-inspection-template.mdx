This is a company level feature.

To delete a form from an inspection you have to login to the company level.

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

First, login to the **Company Admin Area**. Only Company admin can see this button.

<img src="/images/direct/1521177204174-Admin_Website.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.001" />

### Go to **Company Inspections &gt; Inspections**.

Click on the "Edit" button from the inspection you want to edit.

You see the different forms that are used in this inspection

To remove a form, click on the remove button.

Click "Remove" in the warning pop-up if you are sure that you want to delete this form from this inspection.
