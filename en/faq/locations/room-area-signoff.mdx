The option Room / Area Signoff in the menu will give you an overview of the stages of the locations.

You can filter by Location or by drawing, but it's also possible to view a complete list of all the locations of the project

If you click on "View Data" the details will show in the grid at the bottom of the page.

You can print the Location Sign-off by Drawing, Location or Location with details.
