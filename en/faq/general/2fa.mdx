---
title: "How can users activate Two-Factor Authentication?"
---

**Two**-**factor authentication** is an extra layer of security for SnagR application.

It is designed to ensure that you are the only person who can access your account. Users have to provide **two** different **authentication factors** to verify themselves to protect the resource that the user can access.

Once your account is created, you will receive an email notification like this. Due to advanced password security setting , you will have to click **Set New Password** and it will brings you to reset password page.

<img src="/images/direct/1546998798035-Admin_Website.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.001" />

Enter the new password and click **Save New Password**

<img src="/images/direct/1547003461367-Admin_Website-2.002.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.002" />

Click **Login**

<img src="/images/direct/1547003483494-Admin_Website-2.003.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.003" />

At this stage, you have to use your device to generate a verification code. Here are the steps of how you generate the verification code:

Click "**Yes,I'm ready to scan"** to scan the QR code

<img src="/images/direct/1547003519404-Admin_Website-2.005.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.005" />

Go to your app store on your iOS or Android device and install any TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password) supporting app such as Google Authenticator, 2FA Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator.

_The steps below are using Google Authenticator as an example_

<img src="/images/direct/1547000624796-Admin_Device.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Device.001" />

<img src="/images/direct/1547000877329-Admin_Device.002.jpeg" alt="Admin Device.002" />

<img src="/images/direct/1547000897693-Admin_Device.003.jpeg" alt="Admin Device.003" />

<img src="/images/direct/1547003566131-Admin_Website-2.006.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.006" />

<img src="/images/direct/1547001114675-Admin_Website.007.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.007" />

<img src="/images/direct/1547000919526-Admin_Device.004.jpeg" alt="Admin Device.004" />

Enter the 6-digit code and click verify

<img src="/images/direct/1547027039330-Admin_Website.008.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.008" />

You can now login to projects!
