title: "How do I change password?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Once you logged in, tab on **My Settings**, and save your new password.

<img src="/images/uploads/123674-Website_Issue_Mode.001.jpeg" alt="Website Issue Mode.001" />

There you go!

<img src="/images/uploads/127505-sparkles.png" alt="sparkles" width="20" /> Don't forget that **your device works offline**. When you update your password on website, your **credentials on the device do not get to update.**

What to do? When you attempt with the new password, the app should prompt a message "**re-sync users**". Another way is to login to the device with your old password, sync and login with new password.
