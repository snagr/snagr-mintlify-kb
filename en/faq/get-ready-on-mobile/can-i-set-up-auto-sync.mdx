---
title: "Can I set up auto sync?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="20" /> **Device - iOS only**

There are pros and cons to setting up auto sync. On the good side, auto-sync means your device / server gets more real time update. You also do not have to worry about forgetting to sync.

However, once synchronised, only the administrator can delete (mistaken) issues.
