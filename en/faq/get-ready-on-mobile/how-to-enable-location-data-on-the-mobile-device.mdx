---
title: "How to enable location data on the mobile device?"
---

SnagR could record the location of the photo taken, pin at the map and show on the KPI Dashboard.

Before that, users need to enable the permission of location recording for SnagR APP.

To know how to assign the permission?  
Please follow the steps below on your device.

<img src="/images/direct/1559040342760-Issue-Mode_Device.001.jpeg" alt="Issue Mode Device.001" />

Open "Settings"

<img src="/images/direct/1559040379775-Issue-Mode_Device.002.jpeg" alt="Issue Mode Device.002" />

iOS: scroll down to find SnagR

<img src="/images/direct/1559040429999-Issue-Mode_Device.003.jpeg" alt="Issue Mode Device.003" />

Select "SnagR"

<img src="/images/direct/1559040484125-Issue-Mode_Device.004.jpeg" alt="Issue Mode Device.004" />

<img src="/images/direct/1559040520337-Issue-Mode_Device.005.jpeg" alt="Issue Mode Device.005" />

Location access assigned!
