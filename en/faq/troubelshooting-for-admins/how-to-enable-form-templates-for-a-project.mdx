---
title: "I can't see the Inspection Template on the project. What is wrong?"
---

Any Inspection Template created on Company Level is disabled on project level by default.

Don't forget to also enable the inspection template on project level so other users can view and add it.

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Login to a project.

Go to **Project Administration** &gt; **Inspections**.

**Check the box** at Enabled? Column and click **Save**.

<img src="/images/direct/1547104204910-11.001.jpeg" alt="11.001" />

If the inspection template is enabled and you cannot add the inspection still, you may want to check the form permissions at User / Contractor accounts. Learn more [here](/en/set-up-project/accounts-and-permissions-project-level/how-to-set-permissions-forms).
