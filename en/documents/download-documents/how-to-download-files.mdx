---
title: "How to download files?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **Device**

By default your device will **not** download documents that are uploaded to the document repository. If you want to access them offline, be sure to download them beforehand.

When you tap to open a file for the first time, you will be downloading it given there is internet connection. Once downloaded they will stored on the device.

<img src="/images/uploads/127050-Getting_Started.009.jpeg" alt="Getting Started.009" />

<img src="/images/uploads/127051-Getting_Started.010.jpeg" alt="Getting Started.010" />

If you or your admin has organised files into [**bundles**](/en/documents/upload-document/what-is-bundle), you can download multiple files in one go.

Simply go to Bundles and click **Download All**.

<img src="/images/uploads/129304-Getting_Started_Device.011.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device.011" />

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Select the file and click **Download / Open file**.

<img src="/images/uploads/129302-Website_Document.012.jpeg" alt="Website Document.012" />

To download files in batch, [drag files to bundles](/en/documents/upload-document/what-is-bundle), and tap the download icon at bundles.

<img src="/images/uploads/129303-Website_Document.013.jpeg" alt="Website Document.013" />
