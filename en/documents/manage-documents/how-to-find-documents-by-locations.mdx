---
title: "How to find documents by locations?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **Device**

Tap on a location, select Document Repository and you will see the linked documents.

<img src="/images/uploads/129313-Documents_Device.004.jpeg" alt="Documents Device.004" />

<img src="/images/uploads/129314-Documents_Device.005.jpeg" alt="Documents Device.005" />

<img src="/images/uploads/129315-Documents_Device.006.jpeg" alt="Documents Device.006" />

If there are no linked documents, location documents will appear empty.

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Similar to how it works on the app, select a location and go to **View / Manage Documents**.

<img src="/images/uploads/129316-Website_Document.017.jpeg" alt="Website Document.017" />

<img src="/images/uploads/129317-Website_Document.018.jpeg" alt="Website Document.018" />
