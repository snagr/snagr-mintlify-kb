---
title: "How to link documents to locations?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Simply go to Plan Viewer, select the location you wish to link documents to and click **View / Manage Documents**.

<img src="/images/uploads/129305-Website_Document.019.jpeg" alt="Website Document.019" />

<img src="/images/uploads/129307-Website_Document.020.jpeg" alt="Website Document.020" />

<img src="/images/uploads/129308-Website_Document.022.jpeg" alt="Website Document.022" />

Note that documents are linked to a location one at a time.
