---
title: "Who has access to document repository?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/127190-Unknown_Man_Emoji.png" alt="Unknown Man Emoji" width="20" /> **Who** can view and upload documents?

Users with the role **can sign off**, **can close** and **administrator** have access to document repository. They can view and upload documents. Read-only and can-fix users do not have access to the document repository.
