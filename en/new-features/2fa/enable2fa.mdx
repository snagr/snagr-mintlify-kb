---
title: "Enable Two-Factor Authentication (at Company level)"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Two-Factor Authentication can only be enabled by company administrator on company level.

Login to **Company Admin Area**

<img src="/images/direct/1547022828585-Admin_Website.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.001" />

Go to **SnagR Account Settings** &gt; **Security Settings** &gt; **Two-Factor Authentication** &gt; Tick to enable the feature and vice versa

Click **Update Two-Factor**

<img src="/images/direct/1547023127286-Admin_Website.005.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.005" />

Once it is enabled, users from different projects under this company enterprise have to login with two authentication factors.
