---
title: "Release Notes - iOS v4.147.2 (20-April-2020)"
---

- SnagR Application Built against iOS 13 (now required by Apple)
- iOS13 Modal Presentation Style now used for Dashboard & Drawing Finder
- Full Barcode support - You can now scan most Barcode types (previously just QRCodes) to avoid human error and improve integration with external systems & modular integrated construction delivery (MIC).
- User / Sub-Contractor / List search - When filling in forms you can now search to filter the list.
