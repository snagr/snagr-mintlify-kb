It is not uncommon that the same issues repeat over and over again at a construction site.

In SnagR one of the fastest ways to capture an issue would be to **clone issues**. In essence, it helps you duplicate issues of the same kind.

Long hold on the Issue to clone.  
For iOS - Click the pin button  in the menu that has popped up and click “Clone issue” .
