---
title: "What is \"Add Future Issue\"?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **Device**

Add Future Issue allows you to add the details of an issue that will become evident once the initial issue has been fixed.

For example, if damage has been done to a wall and it must be plastered again, it ultimately causes a follow on issue in the form of requiring redecorating.
