<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **Device**

Under **Menu**, simply go to **Dashboard** to view the list of all issues.

At the **search bar**, type in **keywords** to have a filtered list of issues.
