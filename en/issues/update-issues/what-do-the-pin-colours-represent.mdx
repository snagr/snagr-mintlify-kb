---
title: "The issue pins have different colours. What do they mean?"
---

The pin colour / stage signifies that **a team has** **done their part** in the process.

Here are the pin colours you may see:

<img src="/images/uploads/127509-Workflow.003.jpeg" alt="Workflow.003" />

I don't see all colours... Why?

If your current process do not involve that many stages, it is not necessary to use all built-in stages in SnagR.
