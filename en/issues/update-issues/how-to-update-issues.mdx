---
title: "How to update / close issues?"
---

After [an issue is created](/en/issues/add-issues/how-to-add-an-issue), different parties may be involved to fix, sign off and close out issues. It is important these different parties are involved completing the full circle of the issue rectification process.

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **Device**

<iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/108712072" width="100%" height="400" frameBorder="0" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen></iframe>

When you attempt to close issues, you have the option to:

**Quick close**: change defect status / pin colour directly

**Close and comment**: change defect status / pin colour with photo attachments or comments

<img src="/images/uploads/127505-sparkles.png" alt="sparkles" width="20" /> It is best practice to update issues with photos so that it provides evidence and support to the record.

**<img src="/images/uploads/127190-Unknown_Man_Emoji.png" alt="Unknown Man Emoji" width="20" /> Who** can update issues?

Apart from Read Only users, all users and contractors on SnagR are able to update issue records.

**How** to update issues on website?

You can update issues at Issue Dashboard or Plan Viewer.

**Issue Dashboard**

Simply drag and drop the issue to the desired column.

<img src="/images/uploads/123688-Website_Issue_Mode.016.jpeg" alt="Website Issue Mode.016" />

<img src="/images/uploads/123689-Website_Issue_Mode.017.jpeg" alt="Website Issue Mode.017" />

**Plan Viewer**

Simply click Close to update status.

<img src="/images/uploads/123690-Website_Issue_Mode.018.jpeg" alt="Website Issue Mode.018" />

Note depending on your permission, you will see the term &lt;Fix&gt; &lt;Sign Off&gt; &lt;Close&gt; correspondingly.
