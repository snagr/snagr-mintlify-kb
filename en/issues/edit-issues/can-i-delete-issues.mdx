---
title: "Can I delete issues?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **Device**

**Before you synchronise**, you can delete issues you created.

**After you synchronise**, you need the help of an administrator to delete issues.

<img src="/images/uploads/127505-sparkles.png" alt="sparkles" width="20" /> If you have created any issues by mistake, you can take them out before you synchronise.

**<img src="/images/uploads/127190-Unknown_Man_Emoji.png" alt="Unknown Man Emoji" width="20" /> Who** can delete issues?

Only administrators can delete issues on website.

To delete issues, go to Plan Viewer or Issue Dashboard.
