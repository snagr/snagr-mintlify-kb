---
title: "Issue pins overlap on each other. It's difficult to click on them. How can this be fixed?"
---

When issues overlap on each other, it makes it difficult and frustrating to tap on a specific issue pin amongst a cluster of issue pins.

In SnagR you can merge these pins into one, creating what we called a **multi-plot**.

<img src="/images/uploads/127505-sparkles.png" alt="sparkles" width="20" /> There is no limit to how many issues you can add to a multi-plot. But the square boxes <img src="/images/uploads/123301-Screen_Shot_2017-08-03_at_1.25.59_PM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2017 08 03 at 1.25.59 PM" width="37" />that display issue status on the pin can only get up to 5, due to space limits around the pin.
