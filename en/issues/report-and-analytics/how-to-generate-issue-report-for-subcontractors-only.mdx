---
title: "How to generate issue report for subcontractors only?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

A subcontractor report shows the list of issues that are assigned to a specific contractor.

You can choose to export a PDF report or email report to the contractor directly via SnagR.

To export PDF report, choose Print Service.

To email from SnagR, choose Email Service. The contractor will receive an email notification that consists of a link to download the report.
