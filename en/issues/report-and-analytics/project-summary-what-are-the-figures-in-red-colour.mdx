****Late** =** Open issues that has passed the due by date for closure. No one has updated its status since it's created.

<img src="/images/direct/1538040415518-Website-Issue-Mode.034.jpeg" alt="Website Issue Mode.034" />

Note in the project summary above:

Open = **InDate** + **Late** + **Fixed** + **Signed Off** + **Others**

Closed = **Closed**

**What are the red bracketed figures?**

Issues that have moved forward to other stages e.g. Fixed, Signed Off, Other, can still be late, i.e. pass the set due by date for closure.

The figures in red brackets highlight the amount of these issues within a stage.

For example, from the screenshot above, out of the 2574 Signed Off issues, there are 2526 issues that has passed the due by date for closure.
