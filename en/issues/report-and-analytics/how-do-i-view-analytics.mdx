title: "How do I view issue analytics?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Go to **Issue Analytics**.

Issue analytics are standard analysis for every projects. These data are up to date as soon as you synchronise data from the device.

Get the most out of your data by reviewing issue analytics by issue categories, average number of days to fix and close an issue, and contractors etc.
