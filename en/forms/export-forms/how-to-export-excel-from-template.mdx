---
title: "How to export form content into Excel?"
---

This feature requires the administrator to set up an **Export Template** in advance. The template extracts the desired form fields and maps to a desired column in an Excel format.

When the template is created, you can simply follow the steps below.

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Go to **Inspections &gt; Inspection Finder &gt; Batch Download &gt; From Template**

<img src="/images/direct/1521436445874-electric-light-bulb_1f4a1.png" alt="electric light bulb 1f4a1" width="24" />Before you export in batch, you could use the filter(s) on the unified search bar on top.

<img src="/images/direct/1521600778207-Website-Form-Mode_Tra.001.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra.001" />

**Select** an Export Template, and **Export**.

<img src="/images/direct/1521600947891-Website-Form-Mode_Tra.002.jpeg" alt="helpjuice production/uploads/upload/image/3577/direct/1521600947891 Website Form Mode Tra.002" />

<img src="/images/direct/1521600990055-Website-Form-Mode_Tra.003.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra.003" />

You have now successfully downloaded the Excel CSV file.

---

**<u>-Previous version-</u>**

Next, we will transform **CSV file to XLSX file.**

<img src="/images/direct/1521443343223-Mar-19-2018-14-22-36.gif" alt="helpjuice production/uploads/upload/image/3577/direct/1521443343223 Mar 19 2018 14 22 36" />

Open a new blank Excel, go to **Data ＞ From Text,** and **select** the CSV file you just download.

<img src="/images/direct/1521443424032-Website-Form-Mode_Tra.004.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra.004" />

Choose **Delimited** and **Next&gt;**

<img src="/images/direct/1521443446468-Website-Form-Mode_Tra.005.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra.005" />

**Unselect Tab**, **select Other**, and **input** "**|**"

Note | is a vertical bar and it looks like this on a keyboard:

<img src="/images/direct/1521444271954-Screen-Shot-2018-03-19-at-3.24.16-PM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 03 19 at 3.24.16 PM" />

Click **Finish** &gt; Click **OK.**

<img src="/images/direct/1521443618692-Website-Form-Mode_Tra.006.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra.006" />

<img src="/images/direct/1521443641672-Website-Form-Mode_Tra.007.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra.007" />

Here you go! You have now converted the file.
