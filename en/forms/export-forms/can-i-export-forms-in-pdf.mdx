**Export PDF by batch**

Before you export in batch, it would be a good idea to apply filter(s) on the unified search bar on top, e.g. &lt;form name: daily site walk&gt;, &lt;forms created by ...&gt;. This limits the number of forms to be exported in a batch, or you will be downloading all forms that's ever been created on SnagR. 😯
