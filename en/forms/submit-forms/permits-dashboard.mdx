The Permits Dashboard starts with a filter area. Here you can filter What (Inspection), Where (Drawing, Location), Who (Owner) and When (Date).

To filter select the items you want to filter on and click "View Permits". In the list below you will find the permits you filtered. In the list you can see the details of the inspections.

In the first column you can view the permit on the drawing (where the permit is placed) and you can view the permit itself.

You are also able to delete a permit (if you have permission to do so) at the end of the line (by clicking "Delete").

You can also add a permit at this page. Click on "Add new Permit" below the filter area. A pop up appears.

Fill in the details and click "Submit". The permit will be added.
