---
title: "Can I track changes of a form?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Simply open up the form, go to Options &gt; Revision History.

Select the version you wish to trace back to, the areas highlighted in yellow are the changes made to the form at the selected date/time.
