In specific cases, if you would like to trigger a notification manually, here is what you can do:

<img src="/images/uploads/127167-Form_Mode_Device.021.jpeg" alt="Form Mode Device.021" />

<img src="/images/uploads/127168-Form_Mode_Device.022.jpeg" alt="Form Mode Device.022" />

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Step 1 - Inspection &gt; Inspection Finder &gt;

Step 2 - Select the form you need to send the notification, then click "..." &gt; Notify

<img src="/images/direct/1537350432787-Website-Form-Mode.059.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode.059" />

Step 3 - Select User(s)

Step 4 - Submit

<img src="/images/direct/1537350450573-Website-Form-Mode.060.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode.060" />

This triggers an **insta****nt notification** to the selected user(s) via app and email.
