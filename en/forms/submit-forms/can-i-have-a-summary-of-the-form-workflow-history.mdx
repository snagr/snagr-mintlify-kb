---
title: "Can I have a summary of the form workflow history?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

Simply open up the form, and go to Options &gt; Workflow History.

You then have a summary of when and who last updates the form workflow status.
