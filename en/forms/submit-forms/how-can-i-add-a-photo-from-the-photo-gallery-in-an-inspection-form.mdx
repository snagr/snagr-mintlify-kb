---
title: "How can I add a photo from the photo gallery in an inspection form?"
---

In an inspection form, you can add photos by taking a photo or uploading it from the gallery.

By default, if you click on the photo field, it brings up the camera.

If you **long press** on the photo field, you will see the option to upload photos from the gallery.
