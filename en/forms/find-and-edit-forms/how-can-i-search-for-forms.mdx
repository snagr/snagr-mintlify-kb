Simply go to Inspection Finder.

<img src="/images/uploads/123828-Website_Form_Mode.011.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode.011" />

At the unified search bar on top, you can type in **keywords** directly to filter your results. You can add **multiple search tags** to get exactly what you need.

**What** can I search by?  
You can search by form name, who created or closed the form, when the form is created or closed, etc.

**How** can I look for forms in a date range?

Say if you are looking for forms created between 1 Jan 2018 to 31 August 2018.

In the example below, you have typed 1 Jan 2018, select Created _from_ 1 Jan 2018. You then type 31 August 2018, and select Created _to_ 31 August 2018.

The same goes with closed dates; you need to specify the range by inserting a closed from and closed to date.
