---
title: "How can I delete Inspection Forms?"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **Website**

**<img src="/images/uploads/127190-Unknown_Man_Emoji.png" alt="Unknown Man Emoji" width="20" /> Who** can delete inspection forms?

Only when the Form permission and Inspection Permission is "Can Delete", you can delete form on website.

To delete a form, please go to:

\1. **Inspection** &gt; **Inspection Finder**

\2. Click on "**...**" and select **Delete Inspection**

<img src="/images/direct/1521108388151-Website-Form-Mode_Tra22.001.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra22.001" />

<img src="/images/direct/1521108432925-Website-Form-Mode_Tra22.002.jpeg" alt="Website Form Mode Tra22.002" />

Note you can only delete one form at a time.

<img src="/images/uploads/127505-sparkles.png" alt="sparkles" width="20" /> **Beware!: once you have deleted the form, you will not be able to undo it.**
