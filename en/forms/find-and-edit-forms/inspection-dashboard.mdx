title: "How do I get an overview of all Inspection Forms?"
---

At the Inspection Dashboard you can find all the inspections of your project.

Click on Dashboards in the menu and after that on Inspection Dashboard in the sub-menu.

On the top of the page you can filter inspections by What (Inspection), Where (Drawing, Location), When (Date added) and Who (Added by) to find the inspections you want.

The filter applies immediately

Below the filter area you will see the list of inspections

If you click on an inspections title, it will expand and you will see the inspections in the different stages of the workflow ([Web - Add Inspection Workflow](http://support.snagr.co.uk/display/help/Web+-+Add+Inspection+Workflow))

You are able to drag and drop an inspection to another stage, add comment and "Save"

At each inspection you are able to view the inspection on the drawing, see the workflow history of the inspection and view the inspection itself
