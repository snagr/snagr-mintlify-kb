---
title: "How to create an appointment for the Owner?"
---

All the appointment team members are able to create an appointment for the Owner via the Website.

<img src="/images/direct/1597057324784-1597057324784.png" alt="1597057324784" />**Website**

Login to the website, access the **Appointment** page in SnagR Home/FM.

Double click to choose the **Date** and **T****ime** for when would you like to add an appointment.

<img src="/images/direct/1597057728202-SnagR-Home_Website.001.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.001" />

Fill in all the **detail** and click **Save** to confirm.

<img src="/images/direct/1597057738123-SnagR-Home_Website.002.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.002" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597057744303-SnagR-Home_Website.003.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.003" />

Done, you have created an appointment for the owner.

The team member(s) will receive an email of New Appointment as a notification.

<img src="/images/direct/1597058508594-1597058508594.png" alt="1597058508594" />

The owner will receive an email for the information.
