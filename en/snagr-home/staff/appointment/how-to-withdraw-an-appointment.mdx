---
title: "How to withdraw an appointment?"
---

All the appointment team member(s) are able to withdraw an appointment via the Website.

<img src="/images/direct/1597057324784-1597057324784.png" alt="1597057324784" />**Website**

Login to the website, access the **Appointment** page in SnagR Home/FM.

Hover your mouse over the appointment, the red cross will appear on the top right corner, click the **red cross** to request delete.

<img src="/images/direct/1597059692160-4.001.jpeg" alt="4.001" />

Click **OK** to confirm the delete.

<img src="/images/direct/1597059701412-55.002.jpeg" alt="55.002" />

Appointment deleted.

The appointment cancelled email will send to the owner and the team member(s) for reference.
