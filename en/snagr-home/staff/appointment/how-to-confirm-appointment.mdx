---
title: "How to confirm appointment?"
---

Once the owner requested an appointment, the status of the appointment will be Unassigned.

To confirm the appointment, the team member(s) have to assign the appointment to the related team.  
Appointment administrators can even assign appointments in batches.

**<img src="/images/direct/1597061108937-1597061108937.png" alt="1597061108937" /> Website**

Login to the website, access the **Appointment** page in SnagR Home/FM.

**Double click** on the unassigned appointment.  
Select the related **team** to assign.

<img src="/images/direct/1597061260032-SnagR-Home_Website.006.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.006" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597061309072-1597061309072.png" alt="1597061309072" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597061319728-1597061319728.png" alt="1597061319728" />

Done.

The owner will receive an email for the confirmation.  
<img src="/images/direct/1597061581455-1597061581455.png" alt="1597061581455" />

The team member(s) will receive an email of New Appointment as a notification.
