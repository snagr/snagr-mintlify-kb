---
title: "How to the SnagR Home user cancel a requested appointment?"
---

To cancel a requested appointment please log in to the SnagR Home APP.

<img src="/images/direct/1597045072805-1597045072805.png" alt="1597045072805" />**Device**

Click Appointment from the main menu.  
<img src="/images/direct/1597049300566-1597049300566.png" alt="1597049300566" />

Click on the requested appointment and press **Cancel Appointment**.

<img src="/images/direct/1597049315501-SnagR-Home.007.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home.007" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597049619341-1597049619341.png" alt="1597049619341" />Enter the **cancel description** and click the **Cancel Appointment** button to confirm.

<img src="/images/direct/1597049340091-SnagR-Home.009.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home.009" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597049412007-10.001.jpeg" alt="10.001" />

Done!  
The appointment has been cancelled, you could request another appointment if need.

The appointment cancelled email will send to you for reference.
