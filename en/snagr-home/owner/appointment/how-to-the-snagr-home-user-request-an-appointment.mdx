---
title: "How to the SnagR Home user request an appointment?"
---

To request an appointment please log in to the SnagR Home APP.

<img src="/images/direct/1597045072805-1597045072805.png" alt="1597045072805" />**Device**

Click **Appointment** from the main menu &gt; **Create Appointment**.

<img src="/images/direct/1597045112685-SnagR-Home.001.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home.001" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597045127614-SnagR-Home.002.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home.002" />

Select the **Date** and **time** for when would you like to have an appointment.  
_(You are able to request the appointment in a month, the time slot is controlled by the project admin.)_  
<img src="/images/direct/1597045169948-1597045169948.png" alt="1597045169948" />Select **Category** and enter the **Description** if need.  
Then click **Request Appointment**.

<img src="/images/direct/1597045183340-SnagR-Home.004.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home.004" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597045200573-SnagR-Home.005.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home.005" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597045213949-SnagR-Home.006.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home.006" />

You are successful to request an appointment!  
The appointment request received email will send to you.  
Sample as below.

<img src="/images/direct/1597047299554-Screenshot-2020-08-10-at-2.33.39-PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2020 08 10 at 2.33.39 PM" />

You will also receive an email for confirmation once the team accepted the request.  
Sample as below.
