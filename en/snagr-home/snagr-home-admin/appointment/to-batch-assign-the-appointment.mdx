---
title: "To batch assign the appointment?"
---

Only the <u>appointment</u> <u>administrators</u> can assign appointments in batches.

**<img src="/images/direct/1597061899258-1597061899258.png" alt="1597061899258" /> Website**

Login to the website, access the **Appointment** page in SnagR Home/FM.  
Click the **Batch Assign Appointments** button at the bottom.

<img src="/images/direct/1597061933622-SnagR-Home_Website.009.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.009" />

To assign one by one to the different team, you can:

Select the related **team** for each appointment, click **Confirm** to save and send out the confirmation email.

<img src="/images/direct/1597061955068-SnagR-Home_Website.010.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.010" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597061977039-1597061977039.png" alt="1597061977039" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597061988106-1597061988106.png" alt="1597061988106" />

To assign in batch to the same team, you can:

Select the related **team** at the UpdateGrid and click the **UpdateGrid** button to batch updated the assign team for all unassigned appointment, click **Confirm** **All** to save and send out the confirmation email.

<img src="/images/direct/1597062845265-SnagR-Home_Website.013.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.013" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597062857770-SnagR-Home_Website.014.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.014" />

<img src="/images/direct/1597062872458-SnagR-Home_Website.015.jpeg" alt="SnagR Home Website.015" />

Done.

<img src="/images/direct/1597062885048-SnagR-Home_Website.016.jpeg" alt="helpjuice production/uploads/upload/image/3577/direct/1597062885048 SnagR Home Website.016" />

The owner will receive an email for the confirmation.  
<img src="/images/direct/1597061581455-1597061581455.png" alt="1597061581455" />

The team member(s) will receive an email of New Appointment as a notification.
