**<img src="/images/direct/1554367178544-1554367178543.png" alt="1554367178543" width="24" /> What if I have to cancel the form?**

If you have to cancel the form:

**CS:** please change the workflow to **"Cancelled"**

**PI:** if you confirm the form to be cancelled, please update workflow as **"Closed (Passed)"** and put **"Cancelled"** in the **comment box.**
