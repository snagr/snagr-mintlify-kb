Permits are required such that contractors ensure compliance to safety before starting work.

**What were the challenges?**

1. Paper-based process requires contractors to submit permits physically at the site office, which is not very efficient use of time.
2. Permits submitted, approved and canceled are often difficult to keep track of.
3. Tons of paper are used and disposed each day as permits remain valid for one day only.

**Before** Implementing SnagR

To submit and collect a permit, contractors had to drop by the site office each time they were about to start work on site. The permit applications are then piled up to a stack of paper on the table, hoping it does not go unnoticed.

<img src="/images/direct/1537414054155-Success-Stories-Handover-to-Purchaser.024.jpeg" alt="Success Stories Handover to Purchaser.024" />

**How did SnagR help?**

1. Submit and approve permit via mobile app
2. Centralise data and communication by allowing different types of data to be inputted via mobile app, e.g. photos, list of workers, signatures, etc.
3. Paperless solution that allows easy retrieval and archive of all data

<img src="/images/direct/1537417117302-Success-Stories-Handover-to-Purchaser.023.jpeg" alt="Success Stories Handover to Purchaser.023" />

**A****fter** Implementing SnagR

Every morning, the site team is able to keep track of the list of permits to be approved in a summary dashboard, and is able to take action promptly. Once the permits are approved, contractors receives an app and email notification.

**What is required to set this up?**

1. [Permit application form in PDF format](/en/set-up-project/forms-company-level/how-do-i-digitalise-a-form)
2. List of [user](/en/set-up-project/accounts-and-permissions-project-level/how-to-add-users-to-project) and [contractors](/en/set-up-project/accounts-and-permissions-project-level/how-to-add-contractors-to-project) who submit and approve permits (name and email address)
3. [Layout plans or list of locations](/en/set-up-project/drawings-project-level/how-to-upload-drawings) that require permit submissions
4. List of [users who need to be notified](/en/set-up-project/notifications-project-level/how-to-set-up-forms-notifications-for-all-users) when a permit is submitted
