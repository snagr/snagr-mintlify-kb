---
title: "How to generate photo links?"
---

**<u>Issue Photo Link</u>**

https://\{\{ProjectURL\}\}/view-image?type=**issue**&id=\{\{**GUID**\}\}

_Project URL example:_ [demo40.snagrweb.com/training](//demo40.snagrweb.com/training)

_GUID example:_ 551999e8-ae78-410d-8c87-c6ec9f4d64cb

Please get the issue's GUID from endpoint api/defects

**<u>Issue Update Photo Link</u>**

https://\{\{ProjectURL\}\}/view-image?type=**issueupdate**&id=\{\{**PPCGUID**\}\}

_Project URL example:_ [demo40.snagrweb.com/training](//demo40.snagrweb.com/training)

_PPCGUID example:_ 551999e8-ae78-410d-8c87-c6ec9f4d64cb

Please get the issue update's PPCGUID from endpoint api/snagupdates

**<u>Form Field Photo Link</u>**

https://\{\{ProjectURL\}\}/view-image?type=**formchecks**&id=\{\{**FCGUID**\}\}

_Project URL example:_ [demo40.snagrweb.com/training](//demo40.snagrweb.com/training)

_FCGUID example:_ 551999e8-ae78-410d-8c87-c6ec9f4d64cb

Please get the formcheck's FCGUID from endpoint api/formchecks or api/v2/formitems
