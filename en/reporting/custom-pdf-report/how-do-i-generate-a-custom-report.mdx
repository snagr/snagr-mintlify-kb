title: "How do I generate a custom report?"
---

<img src="/images/direct/1560144856691-Website-Custom-Report.001.jpeg" alt="Website Custom Report.001" />

Custom report is a new feature and built by SnagR Business Intelligence team so that it automates reporting for you. It is customised meaning the layout and formatting can appear exactly to how your company has required it to be. Custom report can be exported in Excel, PDF or Word so you can let computers do 99% of the work, and you brush up the remaining 1% if necessary.

\1. Simply go to **Custom Reports** &gt; Specific custom report name, in this case, Handover Inspection Report.

\2. Filters if needed.

\3. Click **save icon**, and choose the format, i.e. Excel, PDF, Word, that you like

How can I ask for access to custom report?

Your project admin has the permission right to give access the custom report to anyone on the project. If you feel you should be seeing the custom report, please get in touch with your project admin (typically a project manager, site engineer, site clerk, etc.) and request access. Click [here](/it/impostazione-progetti/utenti-e-permessi-amministrazione-progetto/how-to-set-permission-for-custom-reports) to see more.

How can I request a custom report?

Please ask the project admin to contact SnagR Business Intelligence team to request the custom report.
