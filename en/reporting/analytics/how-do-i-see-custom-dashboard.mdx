---
title: "How do I view a custom KPI dashboard?"
---

<img src="/images/direct/1537239643406-Website-Custom-Report.002.jpeg" alt="Website Custom Report.002" />

\1. Simply go to **Dashboard &gt; KPI Dashboard** to see custom analytics. Custom analytics are built with the data you gathered using SnagR.

\2. At the drop down list, it displays multiple KPI dashboards that you are given access to.

\3. To export KPI analytics to PDF, you can click **Print**. In the pop up window it should give you the option to save a PDF copy, in addition to printing the report.

How can I ask for the access to KPI dashboard?

Your project admin has the permission right to give access the KPI dashboard view to anyone on the project. If you feel you should be seeing the custom analytics, please get in touch with your project admin (typically a project manager, site engineer, site clerk, etc.) and request access. Click [here](/en/set-up-project/accounts-and-permissions-project-level/how-to-set-permission-for-kpi-dashboard) to see more.
