_1) Issue rectification;_

_2) Forms submission and approval_

_Issues_

#### How do I see all issue created?

On mobile app, simply go to Menu &gt; Dashboard. It gives you the list of all issues created. See more [here](/en/issues/find-issues/how-do-i-search-issues-on-device).

#### How do I create an issue?

You can create issues on web or mobile app:

#### How do I close an issue?

Once you have closed issues, the project team typically receive a daily report for that:

<iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/108712072" width="100%" height="400" frameBorder="0" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen></iframe>

See a step by step guide [here](/en/issues/update-issues/how-to-update-issues).

Don't forget to [synchronise](/en/faq/get-ready-on-mobile/what-does-synchronise-mean) regularly so you see the latest update on your device.

#### How do I generate an issue report?

On the website, simply to go Issue Reporting &gt; [Customised Report](/en/issues/report-and-analytics/how-to-generate-an-issue-report). Apply your filters and click print to PDF.

The above are basics. There are more you can do with issue mode. If you are interested to learn, there are more details here.

_Forms_

#### How do I submit and approve forms?

You can submit and approve forms [via website](/en/forms/submit-forms/how-to-complete-forms-on-website) or [mobile app](/en/forms/submit-forms/how-to-complete-forms-on-device), depending on your preference. When you submit or update forms via mobile app, be sure to [synchronise](/en/faq/get-ready-on-mobile/what-does-synchronise-mean) so that the updates will be visible for relevant parties.

#### How do I find forms or permits?

On mobile app, be sure you are in [form mode](/en/faq/general/what-is-issue-mode-and-form-mode), then simply go to Menu &gt; Dashboard. It gives you the list of all forms that have been assigned to you. See more [here](/en/forms/find-and-edit-forms/how-can-i-search-for-forms).

On the website, simply go to Inspection &gt; Inspection Finder, where you can search for forms by its name, created by, closed by, created date and closed date, etc.
