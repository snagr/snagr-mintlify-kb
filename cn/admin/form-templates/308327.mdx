---
title: "如何编缉已有的名单？"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" />  **网页版**

<img src="/images/direct/1524133634361-Screen-Shot-2018-04-16-at-12.01.24-PM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 16 at 12.01.24 PM" />

首先，请登录公司管理区。如果您不是公司管理员，则不会看到登入公司管理页面。

<img src="/images/direct/1524133672560-Admin_Website_Sim111.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website Sim111.001" />

公司检查模板 ＞ 清单，按图标<img src="/images/direct/1523848840678-Screen-Shot-2018-04-16-at-11.19.54-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 16 at 11.19.54 AM" width="26" />以编缉清单编号（清单名称）。

<img src="/images/direct/1523851105068-Admin_Website_Sim2.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website Sim2.001" />

<img src="/images/direct/1524199537045-Admin_Website_Sim111.003.jpeg" alt="Admin Website Sim111.003" />

或按编缉清单项目以编缉清单项目内容

<img src="/images/direct/1524199552041-Admin_Website_Sim111.004.jpeg" alt="Admin Website Sim111.004" />

按图标<img src="/images/direct/1523848840678-Screen-Shot-2018-04-16-at-11.19.54-AM.png" alt="Screen Shot 2018 04 16 at 11.19.54 AM" width="26" />以编缉清单项目（清单项目名称）。

<img src="/images/direct/1524199570675-Admin_Website_Sim111.005.jpeg" alt="Admin Website Sim111.005" />

<img src="/images/direct/1524199593721-Admin_Website_Sim111.006.jpeg" alt="Admin Website Sim111.006" />

你更可添加清单项目或添加次清单项目。

<img src="/images/direct/1523849567158-exclamation-mark.png" alt="exclamation mark" width="46" />请注意，清单添加后将**不可删除**，只可编缉。
