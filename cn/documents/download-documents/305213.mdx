---
title: "如何根据位置查找文件？"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **移动设备**

点击一个位置，选择文件储存库，就可以查看与位置相链接的文件了。

<img src="/images/direct/1524107457552-Documents_Device_Sim.004.jpeg" alt="Documents Device Sim.004" />

<img src="/images/direct/1524107595198-Documents_Device_Sim.005.jpeg" alt="helpjuice production/uploads/upload/image/3577/direct/1524107595198 Documents Device Sim.005" />

<img src="/images/direct/1524107948114-Documents_Device_Sim.006.jpeg" alt="Documents Device Sim.006" />

如果没有文件与位置相链接，位置文件会显示为空。

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **网页版**

与移动设备上相类似，选择**查看/管理文档。**

<img src="/images/direct/1524108133248-Document_Website_Sim.017.jpeg" alt="Document Website Sim.017" />
