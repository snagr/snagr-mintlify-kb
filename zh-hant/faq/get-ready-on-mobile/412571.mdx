title: "如何授權於移動設備上啟用位置數據？"
---

SnagR可以記錄拍攝照片的位置，於地圖上標記並顯示在表現指標總覽上。

在此之前，用戶需要為SnagR APP啟用位置記錄權限。

要知道如何啟用位置數據？

請在您的設備上按照以下步驟操作。

<img src="/images/direct/1564654715019-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.001.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.001" />

打開設置”

<img src="/images/direct/1564655116492-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.002.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.002" />

iOS - 向下滾動以查找SnagR

<img src="/images/direct/1564655155518-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.003.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.003" />

選擇“SnagR”

<img src="/images/direct/1564655252235-Getting-Started_Device_Tra24.001.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra24.001" />

<img src="/images/direct/1564655391343-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.001.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.001" />

授權啟用位置數據！
