---
title: "用戶如何激活雙重身份驗證？"
---

**雙重身份驗證**是SnagR應用程式的另一層安全。

它設計在於確保您是唯一可以登入您帳戶的人。 用戶必須提供**兩種**不同的雙**重身份驗證**來驗證自身，來保護用戶資料。

一旦建立帳戶後，您會收到這樣的電子郵件通知。 由於進階密碼的安全設定，您必須**設置新密碼**，它將帶您重置密碼頁面。

<img src="/images/direct/1546998798035-Admin_Website.001.jpeg" alt="Admin Website.001" />

輸入新密碼，然後按 “**保存新密碼**”

<img src="/images/direct/1547003461367-Admin_Website-2.002.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.002" />

按**登錄**

<img src="/images/direct/1547003483494-Admin_Website-2.003.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.003" />

在這階段，您必須使用您的裝備取得驗證碼。 以下是取得驗證碼的步驟：

按 "**是**，**我已準備好掃描"** ，以掃描二維碼

<img src="/images/direct/1547003519404-Admin_Website-2.005.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.005" />

在您的iOS或Android裝備上的應用商店，免費安裝任何支援TOTP（一次性密碼）的應用程式，例如Google Authenticator <img src="/images/direct/1566447927939-Screenshot-2019-08-22-at-12.13.11-PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2019 08 22 at 12.13.11 PM" width="33" />，2FA Authenticator <img src="/images/direct/1566448007983-1566448007983.png" alt="1566448007983" width="31" />，Microsoft Authenticator <img src="/images/direct/1566447964813-Screenshot-2019-08-22-at-12.13.17-PM.png" alt="Screenshot 2019 08 22 at 12.13.17 PM" width="32" />。

_以下步驟用 Microsoft Authenticator <img src="/images/direct/1566448031950-1566448031950.png" alt="1566448031950" width="32" /> 作例子_

<img src="/images/direct/1566448064209-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.004.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.004" />

<img src="/images/direct/1566448081676-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.005.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.005" />

<img src="/images/direct/1566448097812-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.006.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.006" />

<img src="/images/direct/1547003566131-Admin_Website-2.006.jpeg" alt="Admin Website 2.006" />

<img src="/images/direct/1566448152415-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.007.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.007" />

用相機鏡頭掃描QRCode

<img src="/images/direct/1566448170105-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.008.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.008" />

輸入所得6位數字，然後按 “**Verify**"(驗證)

<img src="/images/direct/1566448354486-1547027039330-Admin_Website.008.jpeg" alt="1547027039330 Admin Website.008" />

現在您可以登錄項目了！
