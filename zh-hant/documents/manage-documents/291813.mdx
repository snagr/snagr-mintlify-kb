---
title: "如何根據位置查找文件？"
---

<img src="/images/uploads/123669-if_icons_mobile_1564515.png" alt="if icons mobile 1564515" width="18" /> **移動設備**

點擊一個位置，選擇文件儲存庫，就可以查看與位置相鏈接的文件了。

<img src="/images/direct/1520398076808-Documents_Device-tra.004.jpeg" alt="helpjuice production/uploads/upload/image/3577/direct/1520398076808 Documents Device tra.004" />

<img src="/images/direct/1520398112880-Documents_Device-tra.005.jpeg" alt="Documents Device tra.005" />

<img src="/images/direct/1520398134574-Documents_Device-tra.006.jpeg" alt="Documents Device tra.006" />

如果沒有文件與位置相鏈接，位置文件會顯示為空。

<img src="/images/uploads/123693-if_ic_tv_48px_352186.png" alt="if ic tv 48px 352186" width="20" /> **網頁版**

與移動設備上相類似，選擇**查看/管理文檔。**

<img src="/images/direct/1520402689685-Document_Website_Tra.017.jpeg" alt="Document Website Tra.017" />
