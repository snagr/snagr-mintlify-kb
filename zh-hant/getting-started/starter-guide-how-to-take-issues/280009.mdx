<iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/139679318" width="100%" height="400" frameBorder="0" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowFullScreen></iframe>

首先, 請從[App Store](https://itunes.apple.com/hk/app/snagr/id432771189?mt=8)或者[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uk.co.snagr.snagrmobile&hl=en)下載SnagR <img src="/images/uploads/120714-SnagR_app.png" alt="SnagR app" width="16" />。

想要在你的設備上添加項目，要確保你的移動設備連結到互聯網。在你下載你的項目之前，需要設定你的項目和帳戶。

<img src="/images/direct/1520504408415-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.001.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.001" />

<img src="/images/direct/1520504444327-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.002.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.002" />

<img src="/images/uploads/127505-sparkles.png" alt="sparkles" width="20" /> 二維碼的作用

掃描你收到的歡迎電子郵件裡面的二維碼。這樣你的**項目名稱**和**用戶名**就會自動地填寫。 只要再填寫你的密碼就可以啦。

<img src="/images/direct/1520504428675-Getting-Started_Device_Tra.003.jpeg" alt="Getting Started Device Tra.003" />
